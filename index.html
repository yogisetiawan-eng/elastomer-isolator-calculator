ℹ This tool models vertical (axial/Z) compression dynamics only. Multi-pad mounts — especially tall/slender pads on a wide footprint — often rock or wobble laterally (X/Y) at a lower frequency than they bounce vertically, since lateral stiffness comes from shear (typically ~1/3 of the compression stiffness) with no shape-factor boost, plus rocking inertia. If you measure a natural frequency by eye or with a loose test rig, confirm it's actually vertical bounce and not a rocking mode — they're easy to conflate and governed by different physics.
